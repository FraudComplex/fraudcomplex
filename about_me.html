ABOUT ME

I'm about to be a father! I have been married for four years to somebody that I've been with for 10 years, and she is 8 months pregnant today at the time of writing. A big change is coming in my life and it has me thinking about what I spend my energy and attention on.

Apparently, the conclusion that I've reached is that I should be surfing silly little sites on the web and revisiting skills well outside my field to make a site for nobody but myself.

Truthfully, it's about directing my attention and intention. I want to share a world of wonder and creation with my son. The sort of world I imagined we lived in when I was a child, a world that we can still work together to bring about. There have been many little practices that felt impractical in bringing about the sort of world we want to live in - either too small, too unsustainable, too much of a commitment, too solitary. But this feels like a movement of likeminded individuals intentionally building the kind of world they want to live in. So count me in - I'm happy you're here. Let's make some stuff!
